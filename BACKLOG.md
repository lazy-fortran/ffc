# ffc Backlog

This file no longer carries a plan of its own. Everything it used to hold now
lives in one of three places:

- The supported feature surface is `docs/SUPPORT_CONTRACT.md`, which is
  authoritative and is updated in the same change as the code.
- The work order is `ROADMAP.md` in this repository, plus the chunk sequence in
  the workspace plan that orders work across ffc, FortFront, LIRIC, and fo.
- The measurements are `test/conformance/parity_dashboard.tsv`, regenerated by
  `scripts/generate_parity_dashboard.sh`.

The previous contents duplicated all three and drifted out of date. They cited
the `E1` through `E10` epics (#262 through #271) and the #272 compliance
umbrella as live work, and every one of those issues is closed. Keeping a
fourth planning document in sync was the reason the drift went unnoticed.

Standing constraints that used to live here, and still hold:

- Keep `fpm.toml` pointing at `src/`.
- Keep an executable behavioural test for every claimed compiler feature.
- Keep CI green; never weaken or skip a failing test to get there.

The retired MLIR/HLFIR experiment lives only in git history. Reference it by
commit hash if you need to look back; do not revive it without an explicit
decision.
